Assumptions and Limitations

1. Background

The original operational dataset supplied for the Urban Slice Pizza project did not contain ingredient purchasing costs or unit-cost records.

Although the database contains ingredient, recipe and inventory information, the absence of ingredient costs limits the ability to calculate product-level production costs and assess profitability using actual business expenditure.

2. Data Limitation

The following information was not available in the original dataset:

Ingredient purchasing costs.

Historical ingredient price changes.

Actual product production costs.

Complete operating expenses required for net profit calculations.

These limitations are acknowledged to prevent unsupported financial conclusions.

3. Analytical Assumption

To extend the project into profitability analysis, supplementary ingredient-cost data will be introduced using simulated values.

These values are analytical assumptions created for demonstration purposes. They were not supplied, verified or approved by the fictional business.

The supplementary data will be kept distinguishable from the original operational records.

4. Scope of Profitability Analysis

The analysis will focus on:

Estimated ingredient costs per recipe.

Estimated production costs per product and size.

Estimated gross profit.

Estimated gross profit margins.

Ingredient cost contribution.

Potential product-level profitability drivers.

5. Limitations and Interpretation

All profitability findings derived from simulated ingredient costs must be interpreted as illustrative estimates rather than actual financial results.

The absence of complete labour, delivery and other operating expense records also means that net profit cannot be reliably established.

Any business recommendations will therefore consider these limitations.

6. Transparency Statement

The original operational database and its imported records remain distinct from the supplementary costing data introduced for analytical purposes.

This distinction ensures transparency, preserves the integrity of the original dataset and makes the assumptions behind the profitability analysis explicit.
