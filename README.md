# Pizza Business Performance Analysis

An end-to-end business analysis project focused on understanding pizza business performance through business requirements, relational database design, SQL analysis, and business intelligence.

Project Overview

This project explores how a pizza business can use data to understand sales performance, evaluate products, monitor revenue trends, and support better operational decisions.

Rather than starting directly with SQL queries, the project follows a structured approach from understanding the business requirements and defining business rules to designing a relational database and analysing the resulting data.

Project Objectives

Analyze revenue by product category and individual product.

Evaluate product sales volume and revenue contribution.

Identify monthly revenue trends and periods of high and low performance.

Investigate product-level changes contributing to revenue growth.

Develop business insights that can support data-informed decisions.

Project Workflow

Business Requirements — define the business needs and analytical objectives.

Conceptual Data Model — identify the main business entities and their relationships.

Logical Data Model — define attributes, keys, and relationships.

Physical Database — implement the relational database in SQL Server.

Data Preparation and Validation — load and validate the project data.

SQL Analysis — answer business questions using SQL Server.

Power BI Dashboard — planned stage for interactive business performance reporting.

Business Insights and Documentation — consolidate findings and communicate their business implications.

Current Analytical Work

The SQL analysis currently includes:

Revenue analysis by product category.

Identification of top revenue-generating products.

Product sales volume and revenue comparison.

Monthly revenue trend analysis.

Comparison of December 2024 and December 2025 product revenue changes.

The December comparison identified an overall revenue increase of $1,946, which was validated against the product-level revenue changes.
